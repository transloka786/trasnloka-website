# KritRNA public-content register

Updated for the founder-confirmed HBB, DMD and TP53 priorities and Noir review preview, September 2026. This is public writing guidance, not a private research register or a legal opinion.

| Area | Approved public scope | Publication boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Company | KritRNA is the operating brand of Transloka Bio Pvt. Ltd., based in Noida, India | Recognition, certifications and external endorsements require supporting records |
| Research priorities | Selected nonsense-mutation contexts in HBB / β-thalassemia, DMD / Duchenne muscular dystrophy, and TP53 / p53-deficient cancer | CFTR can appear as broader scientific background, not as an active initial programme; no other priority is inferred from disease examples |
| Research stage | Early preclinical research; computational engines under development; programme definition and experimental-validation planning | No completed lead validation, clinical efficacy, approval or quantitative success probability without evidence |
| Therapeutic mechanism | Suppressor tRNA may support continuation at selected premature stops during translation | Do not promise universal efficacy, perfect amino-acid restoration, automatic native-stop selectivity or delivery-independent reversibility |
| Four dependencies | Charging, termination competition, message survival and delivery | Safety, normal-stop fidelity, protein function and cellular response cut across all four |
| Engine 01 | Intended candidate-design and prioritisation capabilities, explainable shortlist, evidence and next experimental questions | Keep implementation, private data and candidate records outside this public repository |
| Engine 02 | Public biological rationale, connected translation processes and testable systems hypotheses | The illustrative network is not proprietary topology or a model-output plot |
| Experimental evidence | Distinguish readthrough, protein amount, protein function, transcript availability and cellular effects | Do not treat different assays as interchangeable or model scores as therapeutic evidence |
| Founders | Dr. Nikhil Bharti and Pragati Bhaisora are co-founders and directors with scientific and outreach responsibilities respectively | Changes to executive titles or biography require explicit confirmation; no invented personal anecdotes |
| Academic research | Nature Communications 2024, 15:2957, DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-47258-9; Bharti and Santos contributed equally | Academic work is not validation of the company platform or evidence of company ownership of university intellectual property |
| Network | Preserve existing approved people and their accurate pending/confirmed statuses | Do not infer an appointment, institutional endorsement or executed partnership |
| Artwork | Public reference molecule or clearly labelled educational illustration | No invented experimental data, model accuracy or clinical outcomes in visuals |
| Outreach and forms | Education and professional enquiry pathways | No patient registration, medical-record collection or treatment-enrolment claim |

Use positive, specific language about purpose, development goals and the evidence being built. Do not introduce unverified “first”, “only”, “best” or competitor-performance claims. Keep the confidential business deck, unpublished measurements, research plans and investment terms out of public source files, prompts and comments.

Removing a passage from the current website does not remove older public Git history. The website update is not a patentability or freedom-to-operate assessment.
